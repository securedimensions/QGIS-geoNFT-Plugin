Cookie statement

geoNFT plugin for QGIS · Version {{legal_version}}

1. The plugin itself

The geoNFT plugin does not set, read or store cookies. It uses no tracking technology: no analytics, pixels, fingerprinting or advertising identifiers. The page the plugin shows in your browser after sign-in (http://localhost:7070/cs-review) sets no cookies either.

2. Cookies set during browser sign-in

Sign in, create account and sign out happen in your system browser, at the geoNFT identity provider on portal.geonft.eu. The identity provider (Keycloak) sets the following cookies in that browser. They are strictly necessary to sign you in securely, so they need no consent. They are not used for tracking or advertising and are not shared with third parties.

CookiePurposeLifetime
AUTH_SESSION_ID, KC_AUTH_SESSION_HASH Tie the steps of one sign-in together and protect them against interference. Until the browser closes, or the end of the sign-in
KC_RESTART Lets an interrupted sign-in start again. Until the browser closes
KEYCLOAK_IDENTITY, KEYCLOAK_SESSION Keep you signed in at the identity provider, so that QGIS and the portal can sign you in without asking again, and so that sign-out can end the session. Until you sign out or the session expires
KEYCLOAK_LOCALE Remembers the language you chose on the sign-in page, only if you choose one. Until the browser closes

If you also use the portal website, it sets its own sign-in cookies (cs_portal, cs_id_hint, cs_oauth_state). They are described in the portal privacy notice.

3. Other servers

The plugin reads data from the geoNFT services, from IPFS gateways and from the OpenStreetMap tile servers through the QGIS network functions. The geoNFT services do not set cookies on these requests. If a third-party server sends a cookie, QGIS handles it in its own network layer; the plugin does not read or use it.

4. Similar storage on your computer

To keep you signed in, the plugin stores your session tokens in the QGIS settings. This is strictly necessary for the service you request. Section 3 of the privacy statement lists what is stored and section 8 explains how to remove it.

5. Removing cookies

Press Sign out in the geoNFT panel to end the identity provider session; this also removes the session cookies. You can delete cookies at any time in your browser settings. Blocking the cookies in section 2 prevents sign-in.
