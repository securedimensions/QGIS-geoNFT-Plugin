2. In short

  • The plugin contains no analytics, telemetry, advertising or tracking. It does not report how you use QGIS.
  • The plugin contacts online services only when you press a button that needs them: sign in, create account, sign out, refresh deployments, show observations, verify against ledger, or open an observation picture.
  • Before the first connection the plugin asks you to accept the terms of use and to read this statement.

3. Data stored on your computer

WhereWhatUntil

QGIS stores its settings unencrypted in your user profile. Anyone with access to your user account can read the access token there until it expires (at most a few minutes to hours, as set by the portal). Sign out when you use a shared computer.

Every connection reveals your IP address, the time and the QGIS version (in the User-Agent header) to the operator of the server. In addition:

During sign-in, create account and sign out the plugin listens on http://localhost:7070/cs-review for the browser to return. The listener accepts connections from your own computer only and stops as soon as the browser has returned, after five minutes, or when you press Cancel.

If you change the service addresses in the QGIS settings, the operators of the servers you enter receive this data instead, under their own terms.

5. Purposes and legal basis

  • Sign-in, listing your licensed deployments, reading observations and verifying them are needed to provide the service you request under the terms of use (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR).
  • Server logs with IP addresses keep the services secure and working (legitimate interest, Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). How long the geoNFT services keep them is stated in the portal privacy notice.
  • The base map is loaded because it is needed to show the observations on a map (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR).

6. Recipients and transfers

The geoNFT services are run by the operator named in section 1. Processors used for the geoNFT services are listed in the portal privacy notice. The OpenStreetMap Foundation is an independent controller in the United Kingdom, a country with an adequacy decision of the European Commission. IPFS gateways are run by third parties named in the observation data.

7. Your rights

You have the right of access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability and objection, and you may withdraw a consent at any time with effect for the future. Write to the address in section 1. In the portal, the Account page lets you download the data held about you and delete your account. You can also lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority, in particular in the country where you live or work.

8. Removing the data from your computer

  1. Press Sign out in the geoNFT panel. This removes the tokens, email and account identifier and ends the browser session.
  2. In Settings › Options › Advanced, delete the geonft group to remove the remaining settings and the record of the accepted terms.
  3. In Settings › Options › Authentication, delete the configuration "geoNFT portal".
  4. Remove the plugin in Plugins › Manage and Install Plugins.

9. Changes

When this statement changes in a way that matters, the plugin shows the new version before it connects again. The version and date are at the top.
